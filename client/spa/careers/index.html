What to expect in the Interview Process?

Intro call

A relaxed first chat with our recruiter or hiring manager. We'll walk you through the role, hear about your experience, and see if it's worth taking further.

1

Practical exercise or follow-up call

Depending on the role, you'll do a short exercise close to real day-to-day work, or a deeper follow-up call. Either way, it's built around the actual skills the role needs.

2

Onsite interview

You'll meet the team you'd actually work with, plus a couple of people from teams you'd collaborate with. Expect real scenarios and questions about how you think, not just what you know. We'll brief you beforehand so you're not walking in blind.

3

Founders interview

A conversation with our founders.

4

Reference checks

Once interviews wrap, we'll speak with a couple of references you provide, mainly to understand how you work best. Usually the last step before an offer.

5

Ready to Apply

Found a role that fits? The process moves fast, most candidates hear back within a week at each stage.
