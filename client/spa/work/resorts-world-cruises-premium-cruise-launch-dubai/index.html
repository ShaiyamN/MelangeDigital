Dream Cruises (campaign ran as Resorts World Cruises): Premium Cruise Launch, Dubai

Introduction

Resorts World was launching its maiden GCC voyage and had to fill cabins in a competitive market with no prior India-GCC cruise demand.

Services

Influencer Marketing

Story Telling

Content Strategy

The Insight

A maiden voyage in a category with no demand needs three audiences served in their own language at once: Indian expats, Arabic locals and international visitors.

Our Solution

To kick off the maiden voyage of Resorts World Cruises (RWC) in GCC geography, we positioned it as more than just a cruise: an exclusive lifestyle experience blending elegance, adventure and GCC-inspired hospitality.


In addition to high-impact print, OOH and digital campaigns, we launched a creator-led experiential campaign with over 44 creators producing content in English, Hindi and Arabic. The content showcased luxury "resort-cruising at sea," exploring multiple GCC countries without repacking, and enjoying top-deck activities.

Business Impact

  • $2.3M+ Attributed sales
  • 10.5M+ Reach
  • 90M+ Impressions
  • 250K+ Clicks
