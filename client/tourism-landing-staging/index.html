Answered, before you ask.

What does Mélange Digital specialise in?

We are a travel and tourism marketing agency specialising in creator-led campaigns, influencer strategy, performance marketing, brand positioning, and primary research. Our clients include National Tourism Organisations, hospitality brands, and travel platforms looking to reach the Indian outbound market and beyond.

How long does a typical campaign take from brief to launch?

It depends on the scope. A focused influencer campaign can go from brief to live in 3–4 weeks. A full brand strategy engagement or multi-market campaign typically runs 6–12 weeks. We'll give you a clear timeline at the proposal stage so there are no surprises.

Do you work with clients outside of India?

Yes. We are headquartered across London, Mumbai, and Singapore and work with clients globally. Our core expertise is in reaching Indian audiences, whether you're a tourism board in Southeast Asia, the Middle East, or Europe, we can build and execute your India market strategy end to end.

What is the Indian Outbound Inspiration Report?

It's our flagship primary research publication, a study of 4,200 Indian outbound-eligible travellers across 12 cities investigating what actually drives destination consideration and booking. It's free to access. Fill in the form in the Research section above and you'll have the full report instantly.

How do you measure campaign success?

We align on KPIs at the brief stage, reach, engagement, website traffic, lead generation, or booking attribution depending on your goals. We report transparently against those metrics throughout the campaign, not just at the end. For long-term brand campaigns, we advocate measuring attribution at the 18-month horizon, not just the standard 90-day window.

How do I get started with Mélange Digital?

Hit the 'Let's Collaborate' button at the top of the page or head to our Contact form. Tell us about your brand, your market, and your goals, and we'll come back to you within 48 hours with an initial point of view.
