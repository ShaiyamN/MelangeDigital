Answered before you ask

What does digital marketing for travel and tourism include?
Digital marketing for travel and tourism includes influencer and celebrity marketing, branded content and IP integration, experiential activations, FAM trips and PR, all built specifically for destinations, DMOs, NTOs and travel brands, not adapted from retail or FMCG playbooks.

What is digital marketing for DMOs?
Digital marketing for DMOs is the full stack of services we offer specifically for destination marketing organisations. We build campaigns that survive government-level approval chains, prove arrivals rather than impressions, and understand procurement realities a typical ad agency has never worked inside.

Does Melange handle influencer marketing in travel and tourism, or only celebrity campaigns?
Both. Melange's influencer marketing in travel and tourism spans everything from mega-follower celebrities to regional-language creators on platforms like ShareChat and Moj, cast by subculture fit rather than follower count alone.

Is marketing for NTOs different from marketing other travel brands?
Yes. NTO marketing means working within a national tourism organisation's mandate, budget cycles and ministerial reporting lines: categorically different from marketing a private travel brand, and a large part of what Melange specialises in.

What does Melange Digital specialise in?
Melange is a travel and tourism marketing agency. We decode the culture, narrate the story, match the creator, and convert emotion into a booking. Success is measured in arrivals, not impressions.

How do you choose creators for a destination campaign?
We cast for credibility inside a subculture, not follower count. We check the data behind every creator before signing a contract, and casting, negotiation, content and reporting run under one roof.
