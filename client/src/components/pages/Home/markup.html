FAQ

What does a travel marketing agency do?

A travel marketing agency turns a destination or travel brand into a choice travellers actively make. It maps the audience's cultural passions, builds a story inside them, casts the right creators, then engineers the path from that story to a booked trip. Success is measured in arrivals, not impressions.

How is a travel marketing agency different from a regular ad agency?

A regular ad agency buys media and hopes it lands. A travel marketing agency starts with why a destination matters to a specific audience, builds the story inside that culture, then engineers the path to a booked trip. The difference shows up in arrivals, not just impressions.

Do travel marketing agencies work with national tourism boards?

Yes. Melange works directly with national tourism boards and destination marketing organisations, including Singapore Tourism Board, Hong Kong Tourism Board, and Saudi Tourism Authority. Our campaigns are built to survive board-level scrutiny: measurable arrivals, clear attribution, and outcomes a minister can report upward.

What results can a destination expect from travel advertising?

Real travel advertising is measured in arrivals and bookings, not clicks or views. Melange's campaigns have driven double-digit YOY arrival growth and multi-million-dollar attributed sales for tourism boards and travel brands. Every campaign is built around a specific, trackable outcome agreed before work begins.

How much does it cost to work with a travel and tourism marketing agency?

Cost depends on the mandate: a single campaign, a market launch, or an ongoing retainer all price differently. Melange scopes every engagement around the board or brand's specific goals rather than a flat package. The fastest way to get a real number is a strategy call.
